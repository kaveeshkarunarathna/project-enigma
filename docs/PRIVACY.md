# Night Atlas privacy policy (draft)

_Draft for the Google Play and App Store listings. Kaveesh should review it,
add a contact email, and publish it at a public URL before release._

**Short version:** Night Atlas keeps your reading life on your phone. There is
no account, no tracking, no ads and no AI. We never see your journal.

## What stays on your phone

Your books, reading sessions, Moon Log, reading paths, journal entries and
quotes, and your settings are stored in a database on your device. They are
not uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app deletes them. You can export your
journal as text from Settings at any time.

## What leaves your phone

- **Book search.** When you search for a book, the words you type are sent to
  the [Open Library](https://openlibrary.org) catalog (run by the Internet
  Archive) to find titles, authors and covers. Covers are loaded from Open
  Library too. Nothing else is sent, and the request carries no personal
  information.
- **Free classics.** When you download a public-domain classic, the app
  fetches that one book's text from [Project Gutenberg](https://www.gutenberg.org).
  The request carries no personal information, and the book is then kept on
  your phone.
- That is all. There are no analytics, crash reporting, advertising or other
  third-party services in the app.

## Camera and photos

The page scanner reads text from a photo **on your phone**, using Apple's
Vision framework on iOS or Google's ML Kit on Android. The photo is not
uploaded, not saved to your photo library, and is deleted as soon as the text
is read. Only the few sentences you choose are kept, as a private quote in
your journal.

The barcode scanner uses the camera only to read the barcode on the back of a
book. No picture is taken or kept. The book's ISBN is then sent to Open
Library to find its edition, the same way a title search is.

## Backups

A backup is a file the app makes on your phone and hands to your phone's
share sheet. You choose where it goes. The app does not upload it, and it
holds your books, reading nights, paths, journal and settings, so keep it
somewhere you trust. Restoring reads a file you pick and stays on the phone.

## Notifications

If you turn on the evening reminder, the app schedules notifications on your
phone. No push service or server is involved.

## Children

Night Atlas does not collect personal information from anyone, including
children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with its date.

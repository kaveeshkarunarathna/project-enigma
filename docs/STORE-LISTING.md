## Full description

Stellibria is a calm reading tracker for the books on your shelf.

Every night you read adds a star to your own sky. Log a session by the time
you read and the pages you turned, and watch each book's constellation fill
in, page by page. Nothing ever resets and nothing turns red: a quiet night is
just a night.

THE LAMP
Start a reading timer, read, then log the pages. Each session counts, not
only finished books.

MOON LOG
A gentle daily goal and a weekly target, shown as moons. No streaks to
break.

YOUR SHELF
Find books by title, author or the barcode on the back. Book facts and covers
come from Open Library.

READING PATHS
Follow curated paths through philosophy, classics, fantasy and science
fiction, or build your own.

THINKING JOURNAL
Answer a short reflection prompt after reading, or save a few lines you loved.
Scan a passage with your camera: the text is read on your phone and the photo
is never kept.

FREE CLASSICS
Read public-domain classics right in the app.

SHARE YOUR SKY
Share a picture of your sky as a story or a square. Your journal never
appears on it.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Everything stays on your phone. No account, no ads, no tracking. Back up to a
file you keep, and restore it on a new phone.
